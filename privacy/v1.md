## 3. The Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the service, improve the learning experience, ensure safety, meet legal obligations, and—where you choose to permit it—support research and development.

### 3.1 Account & Identity Data
- Name  
- Email address  
- Password or authentication credentials
- Parent or guardian contact details (for under‑13s or as required by regional law)

### 3.2 Learning & Activity Data
- User's role: teacher or learner
- Learner's first language
- Learner's learning goals
- Learner's target exams
- Teachers' target
- Writing submissions  
- Audio recordings used for speaking assessments or feedback  
- Reading comprehension responses  
- System-provided scores, progress data, and CEFR-related metrics

### 3.3 Device & Technical Data
- Device identifiers  
- Browser or app type  
- IP address  
- Cookies and similar technologies (see Cookie Policy)  
- General usage analytics  

### 3.4 Research & Corpus Data
Some learner output may be processed for research and development purposes, including improving automated feedback systems and benchmarking language performance. This is explained separately in the:

👉 [Research & Corpus Data Use Statement](/research/latest/)

### 3.5 Children’s Data
If you are a child user or a parent/guardian supporting a child’s use:
- Age or date of birth  
- Parental consent or verification data  
- Restrictions applied based on age
