# Research & Corpus Data Use Statement (Version 1.0)
**Last updated:** *13/02/2026*

---

## 1. Purpose of This Statement

This Research & Corpus Data Use Statement explains:

- what learner‑generated data may be used for research and development,  
- how it is anonymised or pseudonymised,  
- what protections we apply,  
- how consent works, and  
- how you can control or opt out of research use.

This Statement applies to all products in the **&Improve family** (Write & Improve, Speak & Improve and Read & Improve).

It should be read alongside: the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Use & Conditions**:

👉 [Terms of Use & Conditions]({{ '/terms/latest/' | relative_url }})

## 2. What Research Use Means

“Research use” refers to the processing of learner‑generated content to:

- improve automated feedback systems (writing, speaking, reading)  
- develop and evaluate AI‑based language‑learning models  
- benchmark system accuracy  
- understand language‑learning patterns at scale  
- enhance teaching and assessment methods  

Research use **does not** include:

- marketing or advertising  
- profiling for non‑educational purposes  
- selling data  
- sharing identifiable data without a lawful basis  

---

## 3. What Learner Data May Be Used

We may use the following learner‑generated content for research.

### 3.1 Writing Submissions
Texts submitted for feedback or assessment, such as paragraphs or essays.

### 3.2 Speaking Samples
Audio recordings provided during speaking tasks or complete tests.

### 3.3 Reading Responses
Multiple‑choice selections, typed short answers, and comprehension task outputs.

### 3.4 System‑Generated Metadata
- error types and frequencies  
- feedback categories  
- CEFR‑related performance scores  
- timestamps and task‑interaction patterns  

We do **not** use account identity data (such as name or email) as part of the research corpus.

---

## 4. How We Protect Your Identity

Before any data is used for research:

### 4.1 Anonymisation or Pseudonymisation
Where possible, we anonymise learner data. If we cannot anonymises your learner data, we will not use it for research. Data is also pseudonymised using internal identifiers that do not reveal your identity.

### 4.2 Removal of Direct Identifiers
We remove:

- name  
- email address  
- school or institution identifiers  
- device identifiers  
- IP addresses  
- any other direct personal identifiers  

### 4.3 Sensitive Content Filtering
If learner submissions contain personal information (names, contact details, locations), automated and/or manual filtering removes these.

### 4.4 Access Controls

**Public access to published datasets.** We publish certain **anonymised** research datasets on our public website under a **non‑commercial licence**. Anyone may download these datasets after agreeing to the licence terms and completing the required registration form. Datasets that are **not** designated for public release remain accessible **only** to authorised Cambridge research and engineering staff for legitimate research purposes.

### 4.5 Storage Separation
Research datasets are stored separately from operational learning systems, following strict retention and security rules.

---

## 5. Lawful Basis for Research Use

We rely on the following lawful bases under UK GDPR, EU GDPR, and similar frameworks.

### 5.1 Consent
Certain types of research use require your explicit consent.

Where consent is required:

- we will ask clearly and transparently  
- you may withdraw consent at any time  
- withdrawing consent does not affect your learning experience  

### 5.2 Legitimate Interests
Low‑risk, non‑identifiable aggregated data may be used to improve system safety, accuracy, and performance.  
We always balance our interests with your rights and expectations.

We do **not** use identifiable personal data for research under legitimate interest.

---

## 6. Children and Research Use

For users under the required age for independent consent (e.g., under 13), we require:

- parental or guardian approval for any research use  
- age‑appropriate restrictions and safeguards  
- the ability for a parent or guardian to withdraw consent at any time  

Children’s data is subject to additional retention limits and stricter access controls.

**We do not include any content generated by minors in our publicly released research datasets.**  
Where a learner is identified as a child user, their submissions are **excluded** from all dataset releases that are made available for public download under a non‑commercial licence.

Anonymised child-generated data may still be used internally for research or model development **only where legally permitted** and subject to enhanced protections. Such data is **never** included in general‑access public corpora.

---

## 7. Sharing of Research Data

We may **publish anonymised research datasets** on our public website under a **non‑commercial licence**. Access requires acceptance of the licence terms and completion of a registration form.

We may also share anonymised or aggregated datasets with:
- academic research partners
- collaborating institutions
- technical research teams working on model development

We **never** share identifiable personal information, learner accounts, email addresses, IP addresses, or any data that could be linked back to an individual.

Public datasets are licensed for **non‑commercial research and educational purposes only**. No ownership or interest in the dataset transfers to the downloader. Sub‑licensing and commercial use are **not permitted**. Publications using a dataset must **acknowledge Cambridge University Press & Assessment** as the source (as directed on the dataset page). Excerpts of **fewer than 100 words** may be quoted, and datasets are provided **“as is”** without warranties or liability, as set out in the full licence. The **full licence text** is presented at download time and governs your use.

---

## 8. Retention of Research Data

We retain research data only as long as needed for:

- reproducible research  
- evaluation of model performance over time  
- system fairness and bias assessment  
- audit and quality‑assurance requirements  

When no longer required, datasets are anonymised further, archived, or securely deleted.

---

## 9. Your Rights and Controls

You have full control over whether your data is used for research.

You may:

- withdraw consent at any time  
- request deletion of identifiable data  
- request that your learner content not be used for future research  
- access your personal data (subject to anonymisation limitations)  

Withdrawing consent does **not** affect your ability to use the &Improve products.

We will, however, apply your future preferences to any subsequent dataset releases.
